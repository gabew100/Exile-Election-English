# Changelog

## v0.9.1 (beta) - 2026-10-30

- 92 lines reworded after more review passes, for natural English,
  consistency, and accuracy to the Japanese.
- Double dashes (――) are now a single dash (―) in about 830 lines, so they no
  longer look like a gap in the text.
- Debates: about 430 chat bubbles now break their lines more cleanly (they
  left a word or two alone on a middle line), and the inner-voice choices at
  the end of each debate show their full text (some were cut off).
- The election countdown, Election Day, and Debate Start picture cards are now
  in English.
- Menus: clearer map labels, "SFX" in the volume settings, and clearer Game
  Data Install help.
- The download is larger (about 125 MB) because it now includes the
  translated picture files.

## v0.9.0 (beta) - 2026-10-27

First public release.

- English translation of all story text (38,708 lines across 255 scenes),
  menus, settings, scene titles, and the in-game controls and help screens.
- Every line is laid out using the game's own font widths, and every scene was
  checked in the Vita3K emulator.

Known issues: text in some pictures, the two movies, the title logo, the
LiveArea bubble, trophies, and the game manual remain in Japanese. No full
playthrough on real hardware yet.
